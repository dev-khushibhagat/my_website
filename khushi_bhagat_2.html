Bhookh · What I learned

In a one-week live pilot, 14 of 15 registered users placed at least one order, with a 29% repeat-order rate. I also identified and resolved a cart-sync bug that caused stock mismatches at checkout. Post-pilot vendor interviews surfaced the harder problem: workflow disruption, reliability concerns, and no clear incremental upside. Demand from users is not the same as adoption by the business.

03 / My approach

Good product work is
knowing what matters.

I’m most interested in the decisions behind the interface: which problem deserves attention, what the first version needs to prove, and what the evidence says afterward.

01

Get close to the problem

Start with the people, their context, and the friction behind the request — not an assumed solution.

02

Make the trade-offs clear

Turn ambiguity into flows, requirements, a focused MVP, and decisions a team can challenge.

03

Let reality respond

Ship to learn. Pay attention to behaviour, fix what breaks, and be willing to rethink the idea.
