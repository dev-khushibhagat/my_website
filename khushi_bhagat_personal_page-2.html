Founder project · 2025–2026

Bhookh.
From idea to launch.

A mobile food-ordering MVP designed around the everyday friction of campus canteens — menu discovery, ordering, payments, and order tracking in one place.

Product discoveryFigma · 30+ screensAndroid + iOS

I spoke with students and canteen operators, defined the MVP, designed customer and vendor journeys in Figma, and worked with developers through testing, bug resolution, and launch on Android and iOS. The live pilot gave me a chance to observe behaviour beyond the prototype.

“A product can work for its users and still struggle to work for its operators.”

In a one-week pilot, 14 of 15 registered users placed at least one order, with a 29% repeat-order rate. I also identified and resolved a cart-sync bug that caused stock mismatches at checkout.

The hard part after launch

Post-pilot vendor interviews surfaced workflow disruption, reliability concerns, and no clear incremental upside as barriers to adoption. It changed how I think about marketplace products: user value and operator value have to meet in the same workflow.

03 / How I think

Thoughtful products begin
with better questions.

I’m drawn to product work because it asks you to look beyond the feature request and understand the behaviour, context, and constraints behind it.

01

Understand before solving

Talk to the people experiencing the problem. Look for the friction beneath the first explanation, not just the feature they ask for.

02

Make the problem tangible

Turn conversations into user flows, requirements, and a clear first version that teams can discuss, challenge, and build.

03

Learn from what happens

A launch is not proof of value. Watch how people respond, identify where adoption breaks down, and let evidence shape the next decision.
